# Retail Sales Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project explores supermarket sales data using Python to identify revenue patterns, compare product-line and branch performance, and understand customer purchasing behaviour.

The analysis uses Pandas and NumPy for data manipulation and Matplotlib and Seaborn for data visualization.

## Objectives

- Analyze total revenue across product lines and branches.
- Identify the most frequently used payment methods.
- Compare revenue generated by different customer groups and genders.
- Examine customer ratings across product lines.
- Explore daily revenue trends over time.
- Assess data quality by checking for missing values and duplicate records.

## Dataset

The dataset contains 1,000 transactions and 17 columns, including:

- Branch and city
- Customer type and gender
- Product line
- Unit price and quantity
- Transaction total
- Date and time
- Payment method
- Gross income and customer rating

The dataset covers the period from January 1 to March 30, 2019.

## Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Key Findings

- **Product-line revenue:** Food and beverages generated the highest revenue, at 56,144.84.
- **Branch performance:** Branch C recorded the highest revenue, at 110,568.71.
- **Payment methods:** E-wallet was the most frequently used payment method, with 345 transactions, followed by cash with 344 and credit card with 311.
- **Customer type:** Members generated 164,223.44 in revenue, compared with 158,743.30 from normal customers.
- **Revenue by gender:** Female customers generated 167,882.92, while male customers generated 155,083.82.
- **Customer ratings:** Food and beverages had the highest average rating, at 7.11, while home and lifestyle had the lowest, at 6.84.

*Note: Revenue figures represent transaction totals, not profit.*

## Data Quality

The dataset contains 1,000 rows and 17 columns. The initial checks found no missing values and no duplicate rows.
